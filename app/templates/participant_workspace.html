Study debrief

This study examined whether people notice authorization details during ordinary tasks without first being told that altered requests may appear.

Some authorization prompts intentionally differed from the operation entered on the preceding screen. Other prompts were unchanged controls. In post-authorization cases, the attempted execution differed only after authorization. The PoIA server independently compared the original operation, displayed authorization, and requested execution; mismatching operations were rejected even when an altered prompt was approved.

All transfers, statements, and cloud files in this environment are synthetic. You may ask the researcher to withdraw your session record after reading this explanation.
